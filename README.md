## Portfolio Projects

### Analyzing the Factors Associated with Poor Customer Reviews on Olist 

**Code:** [`Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Analysis.sql`](https://github.com/joanfernandez10/Porfolio-Projects/blob/main/OLIST%20E-COMMERCE%20ANALYSIS.sql)

**Goal:** To identify the factors most strongly associated with poor customer reviews and determine which of them represent potential areas for improvement for Olist.

**Description:** The project focused on analyzing the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset, which contains information on orders, customers, sellers, products, payments, deliveries, and customer reviews. The analysis involved data exploration, data quality checks, and SQL-based analysis of factors potentially associated with customer satisfaction, including delivery time, late deliveries, freight costs, product categories, payment methods, installments, order value, sellers, and geographical distance.

**Skills:** SQL, data exploration, data quality analysis, data aggregation, CTEs, window functions, date and time analysis, data analysis.

**Technology:** SQLite, SQL, DB Browser for SQLite.

**Results:** The analysis found that delivery time was the clearest actionable factor associated with customer satisfaction. Approximately 8.11% of deliveries occurred after the estimated delivery date, but around 60% of negative reviews were associated with deliveries that took longer than the overall average delivery time. Other factors, such as product price, geographical distance, payment installments, freight costs, product categories, and sellers, showed weaker or less actionable associations with review scores. The results suggest that reducing delivery times could be one of the most effective ways for Olist to improve customer experience and review scores.
